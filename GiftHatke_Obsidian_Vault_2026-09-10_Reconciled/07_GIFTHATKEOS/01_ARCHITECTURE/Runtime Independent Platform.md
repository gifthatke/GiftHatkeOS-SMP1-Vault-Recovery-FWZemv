# GiftHatkeOS as a Runtime-Independent Platform

GiftHatkeOS is a business platform, not a single technology implementation.

## Business capability areas retained
- CRM
- Customers
- Orders
- Production
- Inventory
- Procurement
- Shipping
- Finance
- Reports
- Settings
- User management
- Roles/permissions
- Documentation
- Workflows/BPM
- Validation/business rules
- operational diagnostics
- digital assets/personalization

## Runtime history
- Google Apps Script + Google Sheets: prototype/reference and original production runtime.
- Standalone cloud: target runtime for performance and long-term evolution.

## Non-negotiable
Runtime replacement must preserve certified business architecture and behaviour.
