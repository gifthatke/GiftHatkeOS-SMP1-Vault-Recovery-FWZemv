# Security and RBAC

## Known security foundation
- Google authentication;
- secure HttpOnly session;
- production RBAC;
- SUPER_ADMIN;
- permission catalogue/role management work from ERP7;
- OAuth production JavaScript-origin correction was required during standalone authenticated smoke work.

## Governance principle
Use authoritative permissions already present in the certified permission catalogue. Do not invent ad-hoc permissions merely to make diagnostics or modules work.
