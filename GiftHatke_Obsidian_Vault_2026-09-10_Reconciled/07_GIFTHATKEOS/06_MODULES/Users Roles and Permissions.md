# Users, Roles and Permissions

Built through ERP7 foundations:
- User Management
- Permission Catalogue
- Enterprise Role Management
- User–Role Assignment

Standalone production uses RBAC and SUPER_ADMIN. New runtime work should map to the authoritative permission catalogue rather than inventing replacement permission semantics.
