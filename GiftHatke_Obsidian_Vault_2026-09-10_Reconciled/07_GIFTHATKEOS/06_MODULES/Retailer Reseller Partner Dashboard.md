# Retailer / Reseller / Partner Dashboard

This is a separate future commercial wave. It is not a replacement for, or
extension of, the closed Personalization or Procurement modules and must not
be merged into Shipping.

## Titan Lock controls

- Basic reseller registration is free.
- Enhanced facilities may require an adjustable ₹2,000 Partner Wallet /
  security deposit; this is not a franchise, investment or guaranteed-income
  fee.
- Reseller pricing is SKU-specific, never a blanket discount.
- Target reseller margin is generally 20–35%; higher margins require explicit
  commercial viability.
- COGS must be verified, user-stated or BOM-backed. Never invent COGS.
- Enforce a safe Gift Hatke price floor, minimum Gift Hatke contribution margin
  and reseller-margin checks.
- Publication status must include `READY`, `COSTS REQUIRED`, `COST BASIS
  REVIEW`, `LOW MARGIN` and `NOT VIABLE`.
- Growth / Pro / Elite volume tiers cannot bypass price-floor or margin guards.
- Registration, catalogue, payment, dropshipping, COD, shipping,
  return/replacement, white-label and blind-dropshipping policies must be
  explicit before live publication.

## Governance status

Planned only. Implementation requires a separate authorization record, exact
file boundary, tests, production acceptance, certification and Obsidian
reconciliation.
