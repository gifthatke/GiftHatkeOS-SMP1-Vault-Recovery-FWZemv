# Settings

Settings/configuration became an enterprise domain through ERP8, including company, organization, finance, marketplace, production, inventory/procurement and notification configuration.

ERP9 is reserved for later Settings & Master Data / Business Operations Expansion **after** standalone v1.0 cutover.
