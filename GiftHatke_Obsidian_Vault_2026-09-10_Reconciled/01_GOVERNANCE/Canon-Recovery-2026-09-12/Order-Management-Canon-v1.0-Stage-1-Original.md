# GiftHatkeOS Enterprise Canon  
## Order Management Canon v1.0 — Stage 1  
### Domain Foundation and System Boundaries

This becomes the permanent specification for the Orders domain across:

- Certified Apps Script Version 1.0
- SMP1 standalone migration
- Standalone GiftHatkeOS Version 1.0
- ERP9
- ERP10
- Future APIs, mobile applications, AI agents, and integrations

The implementation technology may change. These business rules remain authoritative.

---

# 1. Domain Purpose

The Order Management domain controls the complete commercial lifecycle of a customer commitment.

An order is not merely a sales record. It is the central business object connecting:

```text
Customer
   ↓
Order
   ↓
Personalization
   ↓
Production
   ↓
Inventory
   ↓
Shipping
   ↓
Finance
   ↓
Executive Reporting
```

The Orders domain must ensure that every accepted customer commitment is:

- uniquely identified;
- commercially valid;
- operationally actionable;
- traceable across departments;
- financially reconcilable;
- auditable from creation through closure.

---

# 2. Core Business Objective

The Orders domain must answer these questions at all times:

1. What has the customer ordered?
2. Who placed the order?
3. What personalization is required?
4. What amount was agreed?
5. What payment has been received?
6. When must the order be produced and delivered?
7. Which department currently owns the order?
8. What is blocking completion?
9. What materials and production work are required?
10. Has the order been dispatched, delivered, cancelled, or refunded?

No downstream module should invent its own version of these facts.

The Order record is the authoritative commercial source.

# 3. Order Aggregate

The permanent Order aggregate consists of:

```text
Order
├── Customer Reference
├── Lead Reference
├── Commercial Details
├── Order Items
├── Personalization Requirements
├── Payment Summary
├── Fulfilment Dates
├── Shipping Details
├── Operational Ownership
├── Status and Lifecycle
├── Notes
├── Attachments
├── Activities
├── Audit History
└── Revision Information
```

An Order must be handled as one controlled aggregate. Related records may exist in separate database tables, but they must preserve the Order ID as the primary business reference.

---

# 4. Permanent Order Identity

Every order must receive a globally unique, human-readable Order ID.

Recommended canonical format:

```text
GH-ORD-YYYY-NNNNNN
```

Example:

```text
GH-ORD-2026-000184
```

## Identity Rules

- The Order ID is generated by the system.
- Users cannot manually edit it.
- It must never be reused.
- Cancellation does not release the number.
- Archived orders retain their original ID.
- Imported marketplace orders receive a GiftHatkeOS Order ID in addition to their external order reference.
- Internal test orders must still use unique IDs and must be clearly tagged.

The database may also use an internal UUID, but the human-readable Order ID remains the operational business reference.

# 5. Order Types

The canonical order types are:

```text
Retail Order
Corporate Order
Marketplace Order
Replacement Order
Sample Order
Internal Test Order
Manual Adjustment Order
```

## Retail Order

A standard customer purchase through WhatsApp, website, phone, walk-in, social media, or direct enquiry.

## Corporate Order

A bulk or business purchase that may include:

- quotations;
- purchase orders;
- negotiated pricing;
- staged payments;
- GST requirements;
- delivery schedules;
- multiple recipients.

## Marketplace Order

An order received from platforms such as:

- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Meesho
- Etsy
- Shopify marketplace integrations
- future marketplaces

It must preserve the marketplace order ID and settlement reference.

## Replacement Order

Created against an existing order when a replacement must be produced due to:

- damage;
- production defect;
- shipping loss;
- incorrect personalization;
- approved customer-service resolution.

It must reference the original order.

## Sample Order

Used for corporate samples, prototypes, photography, quality review, or customer approval.

## Internal Test Order

Used for controlled system or production testing. It must not contaminate revenue, tax, or operational reports unless explicitly included.

## Manual Adjustment Order

Reserved for authorized correction workflows. It requires an audit reason and elevated permission.

# 8. Customer Relationship

Every commercial order must be linked to a Customer record.

## Rules

- An order cannot remain permanently attached only to free-text customer details.
- When a direct order is created for a new buyer, the system must create or resolve the customer record.
- Duplicate detection must consider phone, email, GSTIN, marketplace identity, and normalized customer name.
- Historical order details must not silently change if the customer later updates their profile.
- The Order stores a transactional snapshot of customer and address information.
- The Customer module stores the current master record.

This prevents old invoices or delivery records from changing when a customer edits their profile.

---

# 9. Lead Relationship

An order may reference a CRM Lead.

The relationship is optional for direct or imported orders but mandatory when created through lead conversion.

## Conversion Rules

When a lead becomes an order:

- the lead remains in CRM history;
- the Order stores the Lead ID;
- the lead is marked converted;
- duplicate conversion must be prevented;
- the customer must be created or resolved;
- relevant personalization notes must transfer;
- agreed value and payment information must transfer;
- the conversion must create an audit event.

The lead must not be deleted after conversion.

# 10. Order Items

Every Order contains one or more Order Items.

Each Order Item represents a specific product, quantity, commercial price, and personalization requirement.

Canonical fields:

```text
Order Item ID
Order ID
SKU
Product Name
Variant
Quantity
Unit Price
Discount
Tax Rate
Taxable Value
Tax Amount
Line Total
Material
Personalization Instructions
Asset References
Production Recipe Reference
Fulfilment Status
Cancellation Status
```

## Rules

- At least one active item is required.
- Quantity must be greater than zero.
- Price cannot be negative.
- Tax must be calculated from the applicable tax policy.
- Every item must retain the product data snapshot used at the time of sale.
- Later product catalogue changes must not rewrite historical orders.
- Personalized instructions may differ per item.
- A multi-product order may generate multiple production jobs.

---

# 11. Commercial Values

The canonical order calculation is:

```text
Item Base Amount
+ Tax
+ Shipping
+ Other Charges
- Discounts
= Grand Total
```

Payment is calculated separately:

```text
Grand Total
- Valid Payments
- Approved Credits
= Outstanding Balance
```

## Permanent Rules

- Monetary values must use fixed-precision decimal arithmetic.
- Floating-point arithmetic must never be used for financial totals.
- The server is authoritative for all totals.
- Client-side calculations are estimates for immediate display only.
- Every recalculation must be deterministic.
- Tax rounding policy must be standardized.
- Financially posted orders cannot be silently recalculated after later product or tax configuration changes.

# 18. Cancellation Rules

Cancellation requires:

```text
Cancellation Reason
Cancelled By

Customer Notification Status
Financial Impact
Inventory Impact
Production Impact
Refund Requirement
```

An order cannot be cancelled blindly after fulfilment activity has started.

The system must assess:

- materials consumed;
- production completed;
- shipment created;
- invoice posted;
- payment received;
- refund eligibility.

Partial cancellation must be supported at the Order Item level.

---

# 19. Revision and Concurrency

Every Order must contain a revision number.

Example:

```text
revision = 7
```

When a user saves an update, the request includes the revision they originally loaded.

If the stored revision has changed, the save must fail with a conflict response rather than overwrite another user’s work.

Canonical conflict message:

```text
This order was updated by another user. Reload the latest version before saving.
```

This becomes essential for the standalone multi-user platform.

# 21. Order Domain Boundaries

The Orders domain owns:

- commercial order identity;
- customer transactional snapshot;
- order items;
- agreed commercial values;
- lifecycle status;
- operational ownership;
- promised dates;
- hold and cancellation state;
- cross-module references.

The Orders domain does not directly own:

- production execution details;
- inventory ledger entries;
- shipment tracking events;
- accounting journals;
- uploaded file binary storage;
- customer master-data history.

Those belong to their respective domains but reference the Order ID.

---

# 22. Mandatory Domain Invariants

The following must always remain true:

1. Every order has a unique Order ID.
2. Every commercial order links to a Customer.
3. Every order contains at least one active item.
4. Financial values reconcile to the Grand Total.
5. Outstanding Balance reconciles to payments and credits.
6. A cancelled order cannot continue through normal fulfilment.
7. A delivered order cannot return to Draft.
8. Production cannot start without required personalization release.
9. Archived orders remain readable.
10. Converted CRM leads cannot create duplicate orders.
11. Every significant state change is audited.
12. Concurrent saves cannot silently overwrite one another.
