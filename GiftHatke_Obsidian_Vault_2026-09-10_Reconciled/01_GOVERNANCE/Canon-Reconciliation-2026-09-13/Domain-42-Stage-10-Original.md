# 10.1 Purpose

Stage 10 consolidates and formally certifies Domain 42 as the permanent GiftHatkeOS architecture for AI Agents, digital workers, bounded autonomy, human approval, tool execution, Agent governance, AI accountability, and future autonomous operations.

14. AI cannot override deterministic business validation.
15. Approval applies to the specific proposed action.
16. Agent execution intent ≠ business result.
17. Tool success ≠ business success.
18. Partial execution ≠ success.
19. Timeout ≠ definite failure.
20. Retry must not duplicate business effect.
21. AI memory ≠ System of Record.
22. AI confidence ≠ truth or approval.
23. Missing data must not be fabricated.
24. Escalated ≠ approved.
25. Agent handoff cannot expand authority.
26. AI Event ≠ Business Event.
