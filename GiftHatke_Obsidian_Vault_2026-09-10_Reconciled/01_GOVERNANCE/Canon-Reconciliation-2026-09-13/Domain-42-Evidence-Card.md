# 10.1 Purpose

Stage 10 consolidates and formally certifies Domain 42 as the permanent GiftHatkeOS architecture for AI Agents, digital workers, bounded autonomy, human approval, tool execution, Agent governance, AI accountability, and future autonomous operations.

The objective is:

> **Ensure AI can extend GiftHatkeOS safely and usefully without replacing human authority, bypassing certified business rules, creating a shadow ERP, or becoming a prerequisite for standalone operation.**

---
