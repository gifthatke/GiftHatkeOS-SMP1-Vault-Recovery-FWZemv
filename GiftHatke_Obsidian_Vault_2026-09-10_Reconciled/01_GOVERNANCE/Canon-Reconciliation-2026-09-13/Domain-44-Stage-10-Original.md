# ENTERPRISE KNOWLEDGE OPERATING SYSTEM & ORGANIZATIONAL MEMORY CANON
## Stage 10 — Final Enterprise Knowledge Operating System & Organizational Memory Certification Canon

# 10.1 Purpose

Stage 10 consolidates and formally certifies Domain 44 as the permanent GiftHatkeOS architecture for enterprise Knowledge, organizational memory, policies, procedures, decision history, lessons learned, Knowledge ownership, provenance, searchability, lifecycle, AI grounding, and historical continuity.

The objective is:

> **Ensure GiftHatke can preserve what the organization knows, why important decisions were made, how work should be performed, and which guidance is current—without confusing Knowledge with transactional business truth or turning SMP1 into a Knowledge-platform programme.**
