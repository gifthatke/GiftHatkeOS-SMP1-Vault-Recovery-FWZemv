# 10.20 Canon Roadmap Continuity

With the completion of the **Quality Management Enterprise Canon**, the next logical enterprise domain is **Shipping & Fulfillment Enterprise Canon**.

Shipping is intentionally positioned after Quality because it governs the controlled preparation, packing, dispatch, carrier coordination, and delivery of products that have already received an approved Quality Disposition.

The Shipping & Fulfillment Domain will define the permanent business architecture for:

- Shipment planning.
- Packing operations.
- Packaging governance.
- Carrier management.
- Dispatch authorization.
- Shipment tracking.
- Delivery confirmation.
- Return initiation.
- Reverse logistics coordination.
- Shipping documentation.
- Cross-domain contracts with Order Management, Inventory & Procurement, Quality Management, Finance, and Customer Service.

This sequencing preserves the TITAN LOCK principle that enterprise architecture mirrors the real operational lifecycle: customer demand, material availability, production, quality assurance, fulfillment, financial completion, and business intelligence.
