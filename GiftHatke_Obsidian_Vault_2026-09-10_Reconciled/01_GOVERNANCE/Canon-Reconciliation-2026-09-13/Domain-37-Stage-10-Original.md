# ENTERPRISE CORPORATE GOVERNANCE, BOARD MANAGEMENT & EXECUTIVE OFFICE CANON
## Stage 10 — Final Enterprise Corporate Governance, Board Management & Executive Office Certification Canon

---

# 10.1 Purpose

Stage 10 consolidates and formally certifies Domain 37 as the permanent GiftHatkeOS architecture for corporate governance, Board oversight, executive Decision-making, delegated authority, strategic oversight, and governance follow-through.

The objective is:

> **Ensure material enterprise Decisions are made by the correct authority, supported by sufficient evidence, preserved with clear lineage, and translated into accountable execution without creating unnecessary corporate bureaucracy.**

---

# 10.2 Certified Domain Mission

Domain 37 permanently exists to:

- structure corporate governance.
- support Board and executive oversight.
- define Decision authority.
- govern Reserved Matters.
- manage delegated authority.
- preserve Resolutions and Executive Decisions.
- coordinate governance Actions.
- manage strategic escalations.
- support conflict and recusal controls.
- maintain governance history.
- connect strategic authority with Project execution.
