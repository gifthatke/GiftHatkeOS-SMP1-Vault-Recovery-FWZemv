# 10.2 Final Canon Mission

The permanent mission is:

> **Measure, control, stabilize, and continuously improve how manufacturing work is executed so that customer demand is converted into reliable good output with minimal avoidable waiting, rework, scrap, downtime, instability, and capacity loss.**

The Domain must enable the enterprise to answer:

- What is running?
- What is waiting?
- What is blocked?
- What is late?
- Which machine or Work Center is constraining flow?
- How much capacity is being lost?
- Why?
- How much output is truly good?
- Where is Rework occurring?
- Where is Scrap occurring?
- Which Standards are inaccurate?
- Which processes are unstable?
- Which improvement should be prioritized?
- Did the improvement actually work?
- Which manufacturing risks are emerging?
- What action is recommended?
- Who has authority to act?
