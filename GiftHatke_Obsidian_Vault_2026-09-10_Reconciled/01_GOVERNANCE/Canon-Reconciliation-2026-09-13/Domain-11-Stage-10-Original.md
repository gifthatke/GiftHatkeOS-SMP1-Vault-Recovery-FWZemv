# 10.21 Canon Roadmap Continuity

With the completion of the **Workflow, Process Automation & Orchestration Enterprise Canon**, the next logical enterprise domain is the **Master Data Management (MDM) Enterprise Canon**.

This progression is intentional.

The eleven certified domains now define:

- The operational capabilities of the enterprise.
- The information governed by the enterprise.
- The coordination of enterprise processes.

The remaining foundational requirement is to establish **a single authoritative source of truth for shared enterprise reference data** used across every domain.

The Master Data Management Domain will govern:

- Customer master data.
- Supplier master data.
- Product master data.
- Material master data.
- Equipment and machine master data.
- Employee and organizational master data (business reference aspects).
- Location and warehouse master data.
- Unit of Measure standards.
- Tax, currency, and reference catalogs.
- Master data quality, stewardship, synchronization, and lifecycle.
