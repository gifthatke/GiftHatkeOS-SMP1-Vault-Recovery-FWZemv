# 10.2 Final Canon Mission

The permanent mission of the Domain is:

> Convert trusted enterprise information into governed performance measures, meaningful management intelligence, accountable decisions, and continuous business improvement.

- What happened?

- Why did it happen?
- What is likely to happen?
- What requires attention?
- Which actions should management consider?
