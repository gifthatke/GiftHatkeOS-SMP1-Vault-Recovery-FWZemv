# 10.1 Purpose

The **Enterprise Innovation, Research & Continuous Improvement Domain** is hereby consolidated into one final authoritative enterprise specification.

Stages 1–9 established:

1. Domain Foundation, Innovation Scope, Research Architecture & Enterprise Improvement Model.
2. Innovation Opportunity, Research, Hypothesis, Experiment, Pilot & Learning Entity Model.
3. Innovation Lifecycle, Research Operations, Experimentation & Continuous Improvement Operating Model.
4. Innovation Services, Research Intelligence, Experimentation Platform & Enterprise Learning Architecture.
5. Innovation Governance, Research Integrity, Experiment Authority & Enterprise Decision Rights.
6. Innovation Events, Research Workflow, Experiment Automation & Cross-Domain Orchestration.
7. Innovation Workspace, Research UX, Experiment Command Center & Enterprise Learning Experience.
8. Innovation Analytics, Portfolio Intelligence, Research Effectiveness, Experiment Performance & Predictive Innovation.
9. Innovation Migration, Research Evidence Reconciliation, Experiment Validation, Testing & Enterprise Acceptance.

- experimentation.
- Prototypes.
- Proofs of Concept.
- Pilots.
- Continuous Improvement.

- enterprise learning.
