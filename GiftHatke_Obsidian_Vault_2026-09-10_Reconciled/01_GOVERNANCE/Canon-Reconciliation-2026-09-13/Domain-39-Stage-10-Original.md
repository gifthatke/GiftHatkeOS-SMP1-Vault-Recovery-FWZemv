# ENTERPRISE COMPLIANCE, AUDIT & GOVERNANCE INTELLIGENCE EXPANSION CANON
## Stage 10 — Final Enterprise Compliance, Audit & Governance Intelligence Expansion Certification Canon

---

# 10.1 Purpose

Stage 10 consolidates and formally certifies Domain 39 as the permanent GiftHatkeOS assurance architecture for obligations, Controls, Evidence, Audits, Findings, remediation, Risk acceptance, certification readiness, and governance intelligence.

The objective is:

> **Ensure important GiftHatke Controls remain provable, failed Controls remain visible, remediation is validated, certification decisions are evidence-backed, and assurance never duplicates the authoritative business Domains it oversees.**

---

# 10.2 Certified Domain Mission

Domain 39 permanently exists to:

- map Obligations to Controls.
- maintain assurance context for Key Controls.
- link Controls to authoritative Evidence.
- record Control execution and failure.
- manage Exceptions and Findings.
- coordinate Audits and assurance reviews.
- track remediation.
- record material Risk acceptance.
- expose assurance gaps.
- support release/programme certification.
- provide governance intelligence.

7. Evidence attached ≠ Control proven.
8. Missing Evidence ≠ PASS.
9. Exception ≠ Finding.

14. Attestation ≠ independent assurance.
15. Audit scope exclusion ≠ compliance.
16. Automated monitoring ≠ formal Audit conclusion.
17. AI analysis ≠ assurance authority.
18. Legacy Evidence ≠ standalone Control proof.
19. FAIL + planned fix ≠ PASS.
