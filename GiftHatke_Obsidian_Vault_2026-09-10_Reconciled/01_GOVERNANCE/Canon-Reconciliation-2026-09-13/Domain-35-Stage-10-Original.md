The **Enterprise Innovation, Research & Continuous Improvement Domain** is hereby consolidated into one final authoritative enterprise specification.

5. Innovation Governance, Research Integrity, Experiment Authority & Enterprise Decision Rights.

# 10.2 Final Domain Mission

The permanent mission is:

> **Enable GiftHatkeOS to discover meaningful Problems and Opportunities, investigate uncertainty, test assumptions through controlled experimentation, scale validated Innovations responsibly, stop weak ideas early, and preserve every important lesson so enterprise capability improves continuously over time.**

---

# 10.3 Final Domain Objective

The Domain exists to ensure that GiftHatke can answer:

- What Problems should we solve?
- What Opportunities are worth investigating?
- Which Ideas already exist?
- What Research has already been performed?
- What Evidence supports the proposed solution?
- Which assumptions remain untested?
- What Hypothesis are we testing?
- What would count as success?
- What would make us stop?
- Is the Experiment valid?
- Did the Pilot actually work?
- What Risks exist?
- Who may approve the next step?
- Should we adopt, scale, defer, modify, or stop?
- Did the expected Benefit materialize?
- What did we learn?
- Have we already tried this before?

# 10.4 Final Certification Principles

Domain 35 permanently follows:

- Problem before solution.
- Opportunity before commitment.
- Evidence before confidence.
- Source before conclusion.
- Assumption before certainty.
- Hypothesis before Experiment.
- smallest useful Experiment before large commitment.
- baseline before Improvement claim.
- Guardrail before acceleration.
- Result before Decision.
- Pilot before broad Adoption.
- reversible action before irreversible investment.
- learning before success theatre.
- negative evidence before selective reporting.
- destination ownership before operational Adoption.
- Realized Benefit before success declaration.
- prior knowledge before duplicate experimentation.
- human accountability before material autonomous Innovation.
