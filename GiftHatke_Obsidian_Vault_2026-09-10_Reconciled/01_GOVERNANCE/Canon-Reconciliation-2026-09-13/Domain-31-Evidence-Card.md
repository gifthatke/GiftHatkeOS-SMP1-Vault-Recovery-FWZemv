# 10.1 Purpose

The **Enterprise Revenue Management, Pricing & Commercial Intelligence Domain** is hereby consolidated into one final authoritative enterprise specification.

Stages 1–9 established:

- Domain foundation.
- Commercial entity architecture.
- Pricing lifecycle.
- Revenue Management service architecture.
- Commercial governance.
- Pricing events and synchronization.
- Commercial workspaces.
- Pricing Intelligence.
- Revenue Optimization.
- Commercial migration and assurance.

Stage 10 formally certifies the Domain as the permanent GiftHatkeOS architecture for pricing, discounts, commercial terms, margin protection, deal economics, commercial approvals, revenue quality, and commercial intelligence.

The Domain exists to ensure that enterprise commercial activity remains:

- Profitable.
- Controlled.
- Explainable.
- Customer-appropriate.
- Strategically aligned.
- Auditable.
- Historically consistent.
- Technically portable.

---
