---

# 10.1 Purpose

The **Enterprise Customer Success, Service Management & Support Operations Domain** is hereby consolidated into one final authoritative enterprise specification.

Stages 1–9 established:

- Domain foundation.
- Service entity architecture.
- Case lifecycle and operating model.
- Service Management service architecture.
- Resolution authority and governance.
- Service events and omnichannel synchronization.
- Agent and Customer Success experience architecture.
- Service Intelligence and predictive support analytics.
- Service migration, reconciliation, testing, and certification.

Stage 10 formally certifies the Domain as the permanent GiftHatkeOS architecture for:

- Customer support.
- Customer Success.
- complaint handling.
- Case Management.
- service requests.
- returns.
- refunds.
- replacements.
- escalations.
- Customer Recovery.
- service commitments.
- Customer Health.
- retention risk.
- support intelligence.
- post-sale relationship operations.

The Domain exists to ensure that customer-service activity remains:

- Customer-centered.
- fact-based.
- accountable.
- fast.
- fair.
- governed.
- cross-domain aware.
- measurable.
- auditable.
- technically portable.

---

# 10.2 Final Canon Mission

The permanent mission is:

> **Resolve customer needs accurately, fairly, and efficiently while protecting customer trust, operational accountability, commercial discipline, and long-term relationship value.**

The Domain must enable the enterprise to answer:

- Who needs help?
- What happened?
- What business object is affected?
- Who owns the Case?
- What evidence exists?
- What is the current finding?
- What Resolution is appropriate?
- Who may authorize it?
- Has the Resolution actually been completed?
- Has the customer been informed?
- Is the customer relationship now healthy?
- What should the enterprise learn from the Case?
