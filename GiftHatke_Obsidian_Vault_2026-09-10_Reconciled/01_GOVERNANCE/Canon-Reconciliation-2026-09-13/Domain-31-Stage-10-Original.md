# 10.2 Final Canon Mission

The permanent mission of the Domain is:

> Govern how the enterprise prices, discounts, negotiates, approves, executes, measures, and improves commercial value so that growth remains profitable, disciplined, transparent, and strategically aligned.

The Domain must enable the enterprise to answer:

- What price applies?
- Why does it apply?
- What discount is allowed?
- Which terms are acceptable?
- Is the deal economically healthy?
- Who has authority to approve it?
- What commercial risk exists?
- Was the final transaction profitable?
- What should improve next?
