# 10.20 Canon Roadmap Continuity

With the completion of the Inventory & Procurement Enterprise Canon, the next logical enterprise domain is **Quality Management Enterprise Canon**.

Quality is intentionally positioned after Production because it governs the acceptance or rejection of manufactured output before inventory becomes dispatch-ready or customer fulfillment continues.

The Quality Domain will define the permanent business architecture for:

- Inspection planning.
- Quality standards.
- Inspection execution.
- Non-conformance management.
- Corrective and preventive actions (CAPA).
- Rework governance.
- Acceptance and rejection criteria.
- Quality evidence and traceability.
- Compliance reporting.
- Cross-domain contracts with Production, Inventory, Shipping, and Finance.
