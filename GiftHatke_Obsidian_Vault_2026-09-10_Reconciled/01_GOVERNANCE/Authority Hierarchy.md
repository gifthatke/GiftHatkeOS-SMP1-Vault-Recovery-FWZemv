# Authority Hierarchy

## Level 1 — Enterprise Canon
The certified **44-Domain Enterprise Canon** defines what GiftHatkeOS is as a business platform independent of runtime.

## Level 2 — Behavioural reference
The permanently frozen **Apps Script GiftHatkeOS v1.0** defines known working behaviour.

## Level 3 — Standalone implementation
Standalone v1.0 implements the same certified business architecture and behaviour using the new runtime.

## Consequence
A faster or newer technology stack is **not permission to redesign business behaviour**. Technical implementation may change; certified business semantics may not drift without a separately governed change.
