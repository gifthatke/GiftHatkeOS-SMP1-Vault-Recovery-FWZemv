# TITAN LOCK — Master Governance

TITAN LOCK is the controlling continuity framework for GiftHatkeOS development and certification.

## Permanent authority
1. **Certified 44-Domain GiftHatkeOS Enterprise Canon**
   - authoritative business and enterprise architecture.
2. **Permanently frozen Apps Script GiftHatkeOS v1.0**
   - authoritative working/behavioural reference.
3. **Standalone GiftHatkeOS v1.0**
   - technical implementation of the Canon with behavioural parity.

## Core rules
- Do not redesign the certified business architecture during migration.
- Do not restart architecture discovery.
- Do not create new domains merely because the runtime changes.
- Do not mutate the frozen Apps Script reference.
- Do not begin Version 1.1 merely to bypass unfinished parity/go-live work.
- Implementation and certification are separate gates.
- Evidence, immutable references, non-mutation checks and repository cleanliness matter.
- Business go-live/handover and standalone parity take priority over speculative expansion.

## Locked post-SMP1 direction
After standalone v1.0 is certified and production cutover is complete:
- ERP9 — Settings & Master Data / Business Operations Expansion
- ERP10 — Enterprise Polish / Final Business Certification
- Version 1.1 and later evolution
- future AI, analytics, warehouse/mobile, multi-branch/franchise/enterprise capabilities as separately governed work.

### Retailer / Reseller / Partner Dashboard wave

This is a separately governed post-core commercial capability. It is planned
as its own wave after the core employee-module sequence and requires its own
authority, implementation, certification, production acceptance and Obsidian
reconciliation. It must preserve the following Titan Lock controls:

- Basic reseller registration is free.
- Enhanced facilities may require an adjustable ₹2,000 Partner Wallet /
  security deposit; it is not a franchise, investment, or guaranteed-income
  fee.
- Pricing is SKU-specific, never a blanket discount.
- Target reseller margin is generally 20–35%; higher margins require explicit
  commercial viability.
- COGS must be verified, user-stated or BOM-backed; no COGS may be invented.
- The pricing engine must enforce a safe Gift Hatke price floor, minimum Gift
  Hatke contribution margin and reseller-margin checks.
- Publication status must distinguish `READY`, `COSTS REQUIRED`, `COST BASIS
  REVIEW`, `LOW MARGIN` and `NOT VIABLE`.
- Growth / Pro / Elite volume pricing may not bypass price-floor or margin
  protection.
- Dropshipping charges, COD, shipping, payments, returns/replacements and
  white-label/blind-dropshipping rules must be explicit before publication.

This wave does not reopen Personalization, Procurement or Shipping and does
not authorize implementation in the current reconciliation.
