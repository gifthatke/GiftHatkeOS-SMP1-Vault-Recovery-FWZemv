# Checkout and Partial COD

Partial COD was explored for the Gift Hatke checkout to reduce COD risk.

## Retained issue
The checkout/app settings visible at one point did not expose the expected “configuration” option.

## Related checkout data
A checkout payload in Aug 2026 showed operational attributes such as OTP-verified phone, COD session identifiers, original total, geolocation/timezone metadata and image URL attributes.

## Goal
Use partial/prepaid collection to reduce RTO/fraud while preserving conversion, especially for personalized goods that cannot be resold.
