# WhatsApp AI Automation

Use case discussed: automate WhatsApp customer service + lifecycle marketing.

## Target capabilities
- order tracking;
- refunds;
- exchanges;
- offers;
- cart recovery;
- review requests;
- related-product follow-ups;
- birthday coupon.

## Retained automation ideas
- abandoned cart: 10% coupon, 2-hour urgency;
- review request: after 7 days;
- related product: after ~30 days;
- birthday coupon.

## Governance
Automations must respect customer consent, platform messaging rules, order truth and refund/return policies.
