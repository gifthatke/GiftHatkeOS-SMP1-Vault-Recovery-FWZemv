# Amazon, Flipkart and Marketplaces

## Amazon
- Live channel.
- SEO, GTIN exemption, HSN/GST and A+ content have been discussed.
- Review copy/assets were also part of marketplace work.

## Flipkart
- Live/order activity discussed.
- Critical operational issue: customized goods can become unsellable if customers cancel/return after production.
- Platform displayed 7-day returns on products in the discussed case.

## Governance need
