# Meta Ads and Creative Strategy

## Creative philosophy retained
- use the actual Gift Hatke product;
- product-first close-ups for conversion;
- emotional but specific copy;
- demonstrate personalization;
- avoid generic awareness art that hides the product.

## Audience groups retained
- women 23–38;
- men 23–35;
- anniversary shoppers;
- parents/family-oriented women;
- best friends/birthday intent.

## Message territories
- Photos Fade. Memories Don’t.
- Not Printed. Permanently Engraved.
- emotional bond;
- “gift is waiting” urgency;
- “5 keepsakes / one bond” bundle value.

## Analysis discipline
Judge ads against downstream Shopify orders and unit economics—not CTR/CPC alone.
