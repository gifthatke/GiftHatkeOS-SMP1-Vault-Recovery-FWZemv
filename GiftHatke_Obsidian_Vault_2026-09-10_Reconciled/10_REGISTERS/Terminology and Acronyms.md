# Terminology and Acronyms

- **TITAN LOCK** — continuity/governance lock preserving authority, sequence and non-redesign constraints.
- **Canon** — certified technology-independent business/enterprise architecture.
- **ERP** — GiftHatkeOS business platform programme/version family.
- **BRC1** — Business Readiness Certification programme.
- **RSA1** — readiness/security-related certification milestone retained in go-live history.
- **GLP1** — Go-Live Programme.
- **OT1** — operational diagnostics/closure programme.
- **PH1** — production hotfix phase used during GLP1 incident correction.
- **SMP1** — Standalone Migration Programme.
- **Production parity** — proof that standalone production matches certified business behaviour.
- **RC** — release candidate.
- **RBAC** — role-based access control.
- **RTO** — return-to-origin, important for COD/personalized-goods economics.
