# Couple, Wedding and Anniversary Products

Known formats:
- personalized couple lamps;
- wedding standees;
- anniversary gifts;
- customized plaques;
- photo-personalized décor.

## Core buyer intent
The product must communicate closeness, memory, uniqueness and “made specifically for us.”
