# Keychains and Small Keepsakes

Keychains appear both as standalone personalized gifts and as components of hamper/bundle products such as Rakhi gifting.

Use as:
- lower-ticket personalization;
- add-on;
- bundle value enhancer;
- compact memory keepsake.
