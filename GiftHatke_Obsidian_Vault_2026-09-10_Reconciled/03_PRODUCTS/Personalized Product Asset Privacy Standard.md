# Personalized Product Asset Privacy Standard

Reusable listing/marketing images must not expose prior customers' personal data.

## Retained editing practice
When preparing reusable product templates:
- remove customer photographs/faces;
- remove names, dates, qualifications or personal messages;
- replace photo/personalization zones with plain blank boxes/placeholders;
- keep the real product form intact.

Examples discussed included covering/removing:
- faces;
- doctor name sections;
- “MD Medicine”;
- name tags;
- beach/customer photos.

## Purpose
Protect customer privacy while keeping the listing image useful for demonstrating personalization placement.
