# GiftHatkeOS Knowledge Base Charter

## Purpose
Build the official permanent GiftHatkeOS documentation suite, synchronized with future releases.

## Audience
- Employees
- Managers
- Administrators
- Trainers
- Implementation Partners
- Franchisees
- Customers

## Benchmark
Documentation quality comparable in clarity/coverage intent to mature enterprise platforms such as SAP, HubSpot, Odoo, Zoho, Dynamics and NetSuite.

## Writing standard
- clear business English;
- explain **HOW** and **WHY**;
- use real Gift Hatke processes;
- separate user guidance from technical/governance evidence;
- keep synchronized with certified releases.

## Scope
Knowledge Base work is documentation work—not an excuse to redesign ERP architecture.
