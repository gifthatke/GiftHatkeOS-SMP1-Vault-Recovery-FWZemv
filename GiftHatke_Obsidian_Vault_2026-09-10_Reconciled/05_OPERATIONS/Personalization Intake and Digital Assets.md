# Personalization Intake and Digital Assets

## Roadmap retained from Apps Script ERP work
- Cloudinary module considered for personalization asset handling;
- unify dual intake paths;
- store asset metadata;
- support asset reuse where appropriate.

## Business requirements
- tie personalization to the correct order/item/customer;
- preserve production-ready asset state;
- avoid accidental cross-customer reuse;
- maintain privacy;
- distinguish customer-provided source assets from reusable product templates.
