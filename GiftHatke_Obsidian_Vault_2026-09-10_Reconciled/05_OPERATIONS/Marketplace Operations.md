# Marketplace Operations

Marketplace operations must account for:
- category/listing compliance;
- GTIN exemptions where applicable;
- HSN/GST;
- product SEO;
- A+ content;
- returns/cancellations;
- proof of customization;
- channel-specific SLAs;
- profitability after fees and return leakage.

Current highest-risk retained issue: personalized return exposure on Flipkart.
