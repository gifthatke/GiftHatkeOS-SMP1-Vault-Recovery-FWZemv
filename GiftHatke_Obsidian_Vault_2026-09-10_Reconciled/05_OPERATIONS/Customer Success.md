# Customer Success

Customer service work includes:
- order confirmation;
- personalization clarification;
- order tracking;
- cancellation/return/refund handling;
- follow-up/review;
- phone/WhatsApp support.

A dedicated Customer Success Executive / telecaller role was designed to support these workflows.
See [[../06_PEOPLE/Customer Success Executive - Hiring]].
