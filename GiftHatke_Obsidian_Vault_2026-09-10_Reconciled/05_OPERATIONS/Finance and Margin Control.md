# Finance and Margin Control

GiftHatkeOS includes Finance as a certified business platform area.

## Margin model should include
- selling price;
- product/material cost;
- shipping;
- payment/COD fees;
- marketplace fees;
- ad spend;
- returns/RTO;
- remake/replacement cost;
- GST/tax effects where applicable.

## Historical platform note
Finance configuration was part of ERP8.5. During staged certification one issue involved rejecting unsafe credential input; later readiness proceeded under the ERP8 final programme.
