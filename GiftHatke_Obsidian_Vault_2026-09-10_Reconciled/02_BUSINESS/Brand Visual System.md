# Brand Visual System

## Gift Hatke platform/UI palette retained
- Background: `#FDF7ED`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` / `#FAF8F3`
- Primary: `#28282B`
- Accent: `#8B6B3F`
- Text: `#1E1E1E`
- Text light: `#6B6B6B`
- Border: `#E7E1D9`

## General visual direction
- warm premium gifting;
- strong product visibility;
- emotional personalization;
- luxury black/gold used for some hiring/print material;
- clean placeholders instead of exposing previous customers' personal data in reusable product assets.
