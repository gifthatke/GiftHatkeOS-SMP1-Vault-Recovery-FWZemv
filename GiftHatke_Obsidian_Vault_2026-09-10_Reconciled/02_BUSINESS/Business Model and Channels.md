# Business Model and Channels

## Direct-to-consumer
- Shopify website: `gifthatke.in`
- Personalized made-to-order products
- COD/checkout operations are important
- Average order value historically discussed around ₹799, while some current products are higher.

## Marketplaces
- Amazon live
- Flipkart used; personalized-return/cancellation risk has been a major issue
- Meesho discussed/planned as part of marketplace expansion

## B2B / corporate
- Corporate gifting opportunities
- Typical corporate budget discussed: ₹5,000–₹10,000
- Product bundles and personalization are central differentiators

## Retailer channel
- Dedicated “For Retailers” page discussed
- Retailer scheme concept: 10 products / ₹10–20k scale, with coupon mechanics discussed in earlier planning
