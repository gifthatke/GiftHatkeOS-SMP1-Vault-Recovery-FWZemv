# Company and Brand Profile

## Brand
- **Gift Hatke**
- Website: **gifthatke.in**
- Tagline: **Unique gifts. hatke happiness.**
- Indian personalized gifting business.

## Background retained
- Business originated after investment in a cousin's gifting shop and acquisition/use of CO₂ laser capability.
- Earlier identity: **Rituals The House of Goodies**.
- Rebranded to **Gift Hatke**.
- Company structure retained: private limited company with two directors (user + mother), with initial contribution notes of ₹50,000 each.
- Earlier investment noted: approximately ₹5,00,000 in the business/equipment context.

## Core proposition
Create emotionally meaningful, personalized physical gifts using photos, names, messages, engraving, acrylic, wood, LED lighting and related customization.
