# Customer Segments and Gifting Occasions

## Retained audience groups
- Women 23–38
- Men 23–35
- Anniversary shoppers
- Parents/family-oriented women
- Best friends & birthday shoppers

## Major gifting occasions
- Birthdays
- Anniversaries
- Weddings/couples
- Rakshabandhan / brother-sister gifting
- Baby / family memories
- Corporate gifting

## Emotional positioning
Repeated message territories:
- “Photos Fade. Memories Don’t.”
- “Not Printed. Permanently Engraved.”
- emotional permanence;
- personal relevance;
- memory preservation;
- a gift that feels “made for them,” not generic.
